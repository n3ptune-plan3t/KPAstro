## Audit checklist for every rule

- [ ] Locate the exact source passage and record book/article, edition, OCR line range and a short paraphrase.
- [ ] Verify the event's primary cusp and why that cusp is judged.
- [ ] Verify every required house conjunction (AND) versus alternatives (OR). Do not flatten conditional logic into a simple support list.
- [ ] Verify denial houses and whether they are absolute vetoes, delays, obstacles, temporary outcomes, or merely modifiers.
- [ ] Verify planet-specific modifiers (e.g. Sun/Moon, Jupiter/Mercury, nodes) and whether they are necessary.
- [ ] Verify the DBA selection rule separately from the natal promise rule.
- [ ] Verify transit activation separately from DBA selection.
- [ ] Check at least one worked example from the same source where available.
- [ ] Add a regression test for the rule and a negative/near-miss case.
- [ ] Set status to `verified` only when all implemented conditions match the cited source. Otherwise retain `partial` and document what is missing.
